Questions about ordering and shipping

How long does it take?

Every piece is printed to order. Allow 5 to 8 working days of production after payment, plus shipping.

Where do you ship and how much does it cost?

We ship to Belgium, France, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, Germany and Austria. Shipping depends on the country and the weight. We give you the price on WhatsApp before payment.

How does payment work?

Once your order is confirmed, you receive a secure payment link: bank card, Bancontact or iDEAL. Printing starts after payment.

Can I return a product?

A piece engraved with your name is made for you and can't be returned, unless it is faulty. A piece without engraving can be returned within 14 days of delivery.

Which colours are available?

Ivory, champagne, brown and black. For another colour, ask us on WhatsApp.
