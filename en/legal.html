Terms of sale

Ordering

You order by WhatsApp message from the product page. The order is binding once DM Atelier confirms the total, shipping included, and payment is received.

Prices and payment

Prices are in euros, VAT included. Shipping costs are given before payment. Payment is made through a secure link (bank card, Bancontact or iDEAL) or by bank transfer.

Production and delivery

Every piece is printed after payment. The production time is shown on the product page. We deliver to Belgium, France, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, Germany and Austria.

Right of withdrawal

For a piece without personalisation, you have 14 days after delivery to withdraw without giving a reason. Return costs are yours to pay, and the refund is made within 14 days of receiving the return.

A piece that is engraved or made to your choices is produced to your specifications and is not covered by the right of withdrawal (Article VI.53, 3° of the Belgian Code of Economic Law).

Guarantee

All pieces carry the two-year legal guarantee of conformity. If a piece is faulty, message us on WhatsApp with a photo.

Disputes

Belgian law applies. In case of disagreement, you can contact the Belgian Consumer Ombudsman Service (consumerombudsman.be).

Privacy

The site sets no cookies. Visits are measured with GoatCounter, without cookies and without data that identifies you.

When you order, we receive your name, your number, your delivery address and the text to engrave on WhatsApp. This data is used only to make, deliver and invoice your order. Invoices are kept for 7 years, as the law requires.

WhatsApp is a Meta service with its own privacy policy. Payment is handled by the provider of the payment link.

You can ask on WhatsApp to access, correct or delete your data. You can also file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).
